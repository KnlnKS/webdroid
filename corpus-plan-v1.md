## Added corpus v1 follow-up: Komikku

Komikku now has a 23-step smoke flow through onboarding, document-tree storage selection, local-source discovery, chapter reading, adding a title, reopening a populated library, and creating/restoring a populated backup. A staged local PNG rendered in the reader, and the flow verified that a nonempty `.tachibk` file was written to private storage. A manual backup decoded to the manga title and its restore worker reported success. The serialized capture reported completed rendering, zero runtime missing APIs or native requirements, and zero layout/draw errors at every step; diagnostics reached the main app and child processes. SQLite logged double-quoted string and automatic-index messages, with no observed failure. A manual browser reload also returned to the library with the selected folder intact. There is no Android golden for this flow, and downloads, broader settings, extension installation, restore into an empty library, and longer-term persistence still need verification. Do not count Komikku among the original 20 results above or as fully usable.

## Verification and evidence limits

- The original-20 boot sweeps rendered 20/20 and collected per-process diagnostics. The last reported sweep had zero called native requirements and 29 distinct boot-path missing entries, mostly optional probes or private services. That count does not describe untouched features.
- Fast-flow matrices reached 10/13 and 9/13 in earlier serialized runs. A later serialized replay reached 12/13 checkpoints in 245.3 seconds; only Doodle Jump missed its retained title frame-rate gate on SwiftShader. The observed genuine platform gap was ChatGPT's `media_projection` service; TikTok still needs guest child-process semantics for `clone` syscall 220. Optional vendor/AndroidX probes and app logcat messages account for many of the 330 reported gaps. This is observed-path coverage, not feature completeness.
- An earlier WebView change routes form POSTs through the proxy. Amazon's product Add to cart sent its body and Android user agent, got HTTP 200, redirected to the cart, and displayed “Added to cart.” A separate simple WebView form POST echoed both fields through the same proxy. Typecheck, all 1,377 tests, and Amazon XAPK smoke compilation passed with zero method failures **before the merge**.
- The earlier Maps GL trace counted sprites and labels but no map geometry. A later callback trace identified the response-header failure. After the generic indexed-header change, both map-style responses completed through the bundled client and map geometry rendered. No probe code was committed.
- Initial launch and feature telemetry cover observed paths only. Inspect each step's `run.json` for `newMissing`, `newErrors`, `nativeRequired`, layout/draw errors, completed rendering, and advancing `wd.ticks`; confirm diagnostics reached relevant child processes. Do not count logcat `E/` lines as missing APIs without evidence.
- The compatibility worktree's 33-app boot sweep rendered 33/33 with complete diagnostics and no required native calls. It exposed a duplicate `Observable` registration introduced during the Komikku follow-up. After consolidating that registration, all nine affected APKs plus Komikku rendered with no `Observable` miss in a targeted replay; the full 33-app sweep has not been repeated after that correction.
